Which Number

Plumbing Versus Water Damage

Before any work, you can verify a contractor's license through the public lookup of the NC State Board of Examiners of Plumbing, Heating and Fire Sprinkler Contractors, according to the board. Plumbing emergencies and estimates use the number above. Water damage restoration and water mitigation have their own line, shown on those two pages. If water is already in the house and drying is needed, see water damage restoration service or water mitigation.

For a gas smell, leave the building and call 911 or the gas utility first. A plumber can help after the area has been made safe.

Before You Call

What To Have Ready When You Call

A few details speed up the call.

  1. 1

    Your street address, including the town and ZIP code.

  2. 2

    What happened, where the water is coming from and whether it is still running.

  3. 3

    Whether you have already closed the main water valve.

  4. 4

    The age of the house and, for water heaters, whether the unit is gas or electric.

  5. 5

    A phone number to call back if the line drops.
