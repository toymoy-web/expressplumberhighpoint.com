How To Use This Page

If water is flowing where it should not, close the main valve and call the line. The emergency plumber service is the homepage service, so there is no separate page for it. Everything below is the detail behind a call: what each service covers and when it is the right choice. Work follows the NC Plumbing Code, which is built on the International Plumbing Code (IPC).

Pages are grouped by problem. If you are unsure, start with the group that matches what you see, such as water on the floor, a drain that will not clear, or no hot water. Each page explains the symptoms, the repair options and what affects the price.

First Steps

What To Do In Any Plumbing Emergency

These steps apply to most emergencies and limit damage while help is on the way.

  1. 1

    Close the main water valve, usually near the water heater, in the garage or crawlspace, or in a meter box at the curb.

  2. 2

    Turn off the water heater if a leak is near it, and stay out of standing water near outlets.

  3. 3

    If you smell gas, leave the house and call 911 or the gas utility before anything else.

  4. 4

    Stop using drains and toilets if sewage is backing up.

  5. 5

    Photograph the damage for insurance, then call the 24/7 line.

Hot Water

Water Heaters

Water heater repair covers dead units, lukewarm water, leaks at fittings and failed parts on gas and electric tanks.

Water heater installation and replacement helps you choose between tank, tankless and heat pump units and sizes the new heater.

Tankless water heater service handles flushing, error codes and lukewarm water on on-demand units.

Drains And Sewers

Clogs, Backups And Sewer Lines

Drain cleaning and unclogging clears slow sinks, stopped toilets and main line backups with cable and camera tools.

Hydro jetting scours grease, scale and root hair from pipe walls when a line keeps clogging again.

Sewer line repair fixes cracked, root-filled and offset sewer laterals after a camera inspection.

Sewer line replacement replaces collapsed or failing lines by trenchless or open-trench methods.

Septic tank pumping serves homes outside the sewer lines, with advice on schedules and warning signs.

Leaks And Pipes

Leaks, Bursts And Supply Lines

Burst pipe repair stops and repairs split, frozen or corroded supply lines day or night.

Leak detection finds hidden leaks behind walls and under floors using acoustic, thermal and pressure tools.

Slab leak detection and repair locates leaking lines under a concrete foundation and compares spot repair with a reroute.

Water line repair and replacement deals with the buried line between the meter and the house.

Repiping and whole-home pipe replacement replaces galvanized, polybutylene or failing copper supply lines.

Water Damage

After The Leak

Water damage restoration extracts water and dries and cleans wet structure, on its own dedicated line.

Water mitigation covers the first hours of damage control, such as removing water and protecting belongings.

Sump pump installation and repair keeps groundwater out of basements and crawlspaces.

Fixtures

Toilets, Faucets, Disposals And Showers

Toilet repair and installation fixes running, leaking and rocking toilets and installs new ones.

Faucet and sink repair covers drips, weak flow, under-sink leaks and new faucet installs.

Garbage disposal repair and installation clears jams, fixes leaks and installs new units, with septic-safe advice.

Shower and bathtub installation replaces tubs, showers and valves with code-compliant plumbing.

Gas, Water Quality And Business

Specialty Services

Gas line installation and repair adds and fixes lines for ranges, dryers, fireplaces and grills.

Backflow testing and prevention tests, repairs and installs the assemblies that protect drinking water.

Water softener installation and repair includes an honest check on whether city water needs one.

Water filtration installation covers whole-home and under-sink filters chosen from your water's actual issues.

Commercial plumbing serves restaurants, showrooms, offices and rental buildings.
