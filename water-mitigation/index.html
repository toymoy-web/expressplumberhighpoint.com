Two Stages

Mitigation Versus Restoration

Mitigation (the first hours)

  • Stop the spread of water
  • Remove standing water
  • Protect furniture and belongings
  • Start drying and take readings

Restoration (the full recovery)

  • Dry out the whole structure
  • Clean and treat affected materials
  • Replace what cannot be saved
  • Rebuild finishes. See water damage restoration.
Emergency Mitigation

Emergency Water Mitigation: What Happens On Arrival

On arrival, a mitigation crew finds the wet area with moisture meters, removes standing water and decides what can be dried in place. Materials that cannot be saved are cut out so the structure can dry. The crew sets drying equipment and returns to check the readings.

Call as soon as the water is stopped. The sooner mitigation begins, the smaller the area that needs removal. Share when the water started and what it touched so the crew can plan equipment and staffing.

Why Fast

Mitigation Is About Limiting The Damage

Water moves in every direction. It pulls up walls by wicking, drops through ceilings and spreads under flooring far beyond the visible wet spot. Mitigation is the set of actions taken early to slow that spread so the later repair stays smaller. Waiting for an adjuster or a convenient day can turn a floor repair into a wall and ceiling repair.

The ANSI/IICRC S500 standard for professional water damage restoration and the EPA's advice to dry wet materials within 24 to 48 hours both treat the early hours as the window that matters. This describes an industry standard and is not a claim that this business holds a certification.

Many homeowners policies also expect you to take reasonable steps to prevent further loss after damage occurs. Mitigation is how you show you did. Keep notes and receipts for every step.

Do This First

What To Do In The First Hour After Water Gets In

These steps keep you safe and reduce the work needed later.

  1. 1

    Stop the source. Close the main water valve if a pipe is leaking, or call a plumber for a burst line.

  2. 2

    Switch off power only if the panel is dry, and never touch outlets while standing in water.

  3. 3

    Take photos and short videos of every wet area, the source and damaged belongings.

  4. 4

    Move small items, rugs and electronics to a dry room if it is safe.

  5. 5

    Lift upholstered furniture off wet carpet with foil or plastic blocks under the legs.

  6. 6

    Call this line and tell the dispatcher what is wet and how long it has been.

Local Note

Where Water Hides In Older High Point Houses

Houses built in the Uptown Suburbs between 1903 and 1963 and the mill houses around Highland Cotton Mills often have hardwood floors over crawlspaces. Water that soaks through the floor can sit in the crawlspace insulation unseen. Moisture mapping looks there as well.

In south-side slab ranches, a supply line leak can wet the slab edge and carpet tack strips for days before it appears. If you cannot tell where the water is coming from, electronic leak detection finds the plumbing source, and burst pipe service fixes a failed line. Calls come in from Thomasville and the other towns on the map as well.
