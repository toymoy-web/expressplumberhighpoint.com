Cost Of A Drip

A Dripping Faucet Wastes More Than You Expect

The EPA's WaterSense program says a faucet that drips once per second can waste more than 3,000 gallons a year. That is water you pay for twice, since it also goes through the sewer charge. A failing faucet usually needs a cartridge, a washer or a seat, all of which are modest repairs.

Older fixtures can be harder than they look. Parts for discontinued models may be unavailable, and a faucet that has corroded onto its mounting can be slow to remove. In those cases, replacement can be the better value.

Emergency Sink Leaks

Emergency Faucet And Sink Repair: Water You Cannot Stop

A faucet or sink problem becomes an emergency when water will not stop. A burst supply line, a failed shutoff valve or a cracked drain trap can all move gallons in minutes. Close the small valves under the sink first, then the main valve if the leak keeps going.

A stuck or snapped angle valve is a common reason people cannot stop the water. Do not force a valve that will not turn. Close the main and call the emergency line, since the plumber can replace the valve and the line in one visit.

Before Calling

What To Do When A Faucet Or Sink Leaks

A few steps limit damage and help the plumber.

  1. 1

    Close the two small shutoff valves under the sink by turning them clockwise. If they will not turn, close the main valve.

  2. 2

    Place a bucket under the leak and take out items stored in the cabinet.

  3. 3

    Dry the cabinet floor and check whether the wood is swollen or soft.

  4. 4

    Look for the source: handle, spout base, supply line, slip nut or drain.

  5. 5

    Take a photo of the faucet and the label under the sink for the brand and model.

Decision

Repair Or Replace The Faucet

Repair is the better choice when

  • The faucet is a recent, common model
  • The finish is in good shape
  • The problem is a cartridge or O-ring
  • Parts are easy to find

Replace is the better choice when

  • The faucet is old or discontinued
  • The body or finish is pitted or corroded
  • You want a WaterSense model that uses less water
  • Repairs are repeating
Water Savings

WaterSense Faucets And Aerators

WaterSense labeled bathroom faucets and aerators flow at 1.5 gallons per minute or less, compared with the 2.2 gallons per minute federal maximum. They still feel strong because the aerator mixes air into the stream. Where you are replacing a worn faucet, a WaterSense model is a low-cost upgrade.

If the shutoff valves under the sink are old and stuck, replace them when the faucet comes out. Failed shutoff valves are the reason a small job turns into a call about the main water valve.

Local Notes

Faucet And Sink Issues In Older High Point Houses

Houses in the Uptown Suburbs and the mill village often have original galvanized supply stubs behind the sink cabinet, which corrode and restrict flow. When a new faucet seems weak right out of the box, the supply line behind the wall is often the cause. A visit may turn into a conversation about repiping.

Kitchen sinks also connect to disposals and dishwashers. If the disposal is jammed or leaking below the sink, see garbage disposal repair and installation. A bathroom remodel that adds a new vanity, tub or shower may fall under shower and bathtub installation.

Valves And Spigots

Broken Supply Valves, Shower Valves And Outdoor Spigots

Fixture calls often start with a part that has failed, such as a snapped angle valve, a shower valve that drips behind the wall or an outdoor spigot that leaks inside the wall. Each one has a different fix, and some need access to the wall. Say where the water appears and what the handle does.

Utility and laundry sinks add hot and cold supply valves that corrode. A leaking valve behind a washing machine is a good example. Replace a valve before it fails instead of after, because a quarter-turn valve is cheap compared to a flooded laundry room.
