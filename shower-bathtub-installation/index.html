Scope

Where The Plumber's Work Fits In A Bathroom Remodel

Shower and tub installation covers the plumbing a remodel depends on: the valve, the drain and trap, the supply lines to the tub spout and showerhead, and the connections to the wall or pan. Tile, waterproofing and finish carpentry may be done by a separate trade, so ask who owns each step before work starts.

A tub swap in the same location is simpler than moving the drain or building a new walk-in shower. Moving the drain means working in the floor or slab, which changes both cost and schedule.

Code Items

What The Plumbing Code Requires

The NC Plumbing Code follows the International Plumbing Code (IPC). Shower valves must be pressure-balance or thermostatic mixing types, which keep the water from turning scalding if someone flushes a toilet. A shower compartment generally needs a finished area of at least 900 square inches and a 30-inch minimum dimension.

Drains need an accessible trap and a correct vent. Set the water heater near 120 degrees Fahrenheit, which is widely recommended to reduce scald risk. Permits may apply to tub or shower installation, and the plumber should confirm with the local inspections office.

Hidden Leaks

What To Do If A Shower Leaks Behind The Wall

A shower leak that stays hidden can rot framing and feed mold.

  1. 1

    Stop using the shower and close the main valve if water is running inside the wall.

  2. 2

    Check the ceiling and walls below and beside the shower for stains or swelling.

  3. 3

    Look for loose tiles, cracked grout or a soft spot at the tub edge.

  4. 4

    Keep people away from sagging ceilings, which may be holding water.

  5. 5

    Photograph the damage and call a plumber to test the valve, pan and drain.

Older Houses

What Plumbers Find Behind Older High Point Bathrooms

In houses from the Uptown Suburbs district and the mill village, bathrooms often sit over crawlspaces. Floors may be soft near the tub from years of seepage, and cast-iron drains may be corroded. A bathroom remodel is a good time to replace old galvanized supply stubs, which can restrict flow and make the new showerhead feel weak.

South-side ranches usually have drain lines under the slab, so relocating a tub means cutting concrete. If a leak has already damaged the wall or floor, professional water damage restoration addresses that, and electronic leak detection can locate the plumbing source. Pair a remodel with faucet and sink work and toilet installation to finish the room in one visit.
