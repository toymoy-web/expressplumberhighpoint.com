About The Village

Plumbing In Highland Mill Village, High Point

Highland Mill Village is a residential neighborhood in the southwest part of downtown High Point. It was built from 1913 around the Highland Cotton Mills, and the National Register of Historic Places listed it in 2014. The city says it is still a residential neighborhood with an active neighborhood association.

The district covers about 69 acres and nine streets. Most of its contributing buildings are mill houses, according to the National Register summary. Call the line when a leak, a clog or a cold shower needs a plumber at a village address.

Hot Water

When The Water Heater Stops Working

The most common household complaint in any neighborhood is also the easiest to check first. If there is no hot water, look at the breaker for an electric heater or the pilot light for a gas one. A tripped breaker or an out pilot has a simple fix, and anything else needs a plumber.

Water around the base of the tank is different. Turn off the supply valve on top of the tank and the power or gas, then call. A tank that leaks from the shell cannot be patched and usually means a replacement.

Addresses

Four Things That Help A Plumber Find You

  1. 1

    Use the full street name

    Several village streets are Places, not Streets. Say Fowler Place or Culler Place in full.

  2. 2

    Name the nearest main street

    Mill Avenue and South Elm Street are the main routes in the district boundary.

  3. 3

    Say where the shutoff is

    Tell the plumber where the main valve or meter box sits, if you know.

  4. 4

    Mention access

    If the water heater is in a tight closet or low space, say so. It tells the plumber what to bring.

Plumbing Services For Village Homes

These are general plumbing services and not a record of calls in the village. A tank that fails is a water heater service or water heater replacement service job, and a tank that is nearing the end of its life can be swapped on a planned schedule. A moving meter with nothing running calls for leak detection service.

Highland Cotton Mills Village is a National Register district and not one of the city's four local districts. The city's certificate rule for exterior changes applies to local districts, so ask the city if you are unsure about a job that changes the outside of your house.
