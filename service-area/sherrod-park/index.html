About Sherrod Park

Plumbing In Sherrod Park, High Point

Sherrod Park is a small residential historic district in High Point, built along the 200 and 300 blocks of Woodrow Avenue. It is listed on the National Register of Historic Places and is also one of four local historic districts regulated by the City of High Point. Despite the name, it is a neighborhood and not a park.

Development ran from 1926 to 1941, and the district covers about 18 acres. The city counts about 66 properties along Woodrow Avenue from North Hamilton Street to Forrest Street. The listed styles are Colonial Revival, Bungalow and Craftsman, and Tudor Revival.

Exterior Review

Inside Work And Outside Work In A Local Historic District

The city requires a certificate of appropriateness before an owner changes the exterior of a property in a local historic district. Its list of covered work includes altering or restoring a building, major landscaping changes, removing mature trees, and fences, walls, decks, steps and exterior lighting. Staff can approve some minor work, such as minor repairs and like-for-like replacements, without a full commission review.

The city page does not mention plumbing. Interior features are outside the review, so a repair under a sink or behind a wall is a different matter from work that reaches the yard or the street side. If a job means digging in the yard or changing anything visible from outside, ask the city first and tell your plumber the answer.

Planning The Job

Four Steps Before Plumbing Work Starts In Sherrod Park

  1. 1

    Decide if the job reaches outside

    A leaking supply line under a bathroom sink stays inside. A water line to the street or a sewer lateral under the yard does not.

  2. 2

    Ask the city early

    The city asks owners to call its historic preservation staff before any work that affects the exterior. Ask whether the job needs a certificate and which type.

  3. 3

    Share the answer with your plumber

    Tell the plumber what the city said so the schedule allows for any approval time.

  4. 4

    Keep the paperwork

    File the city's reply and the repair estimate together. They help if you sell the house or file an insurance claim.
