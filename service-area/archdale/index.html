Emergency Plumber In Archdale, NC 27263: Why Clay Soil Shapes The Calls

Archdale is a city of roughly 11,900 people just south of High Point, and it exists in its current form partly because of the ground under it. Local history records that the area's heavy clay was a reason water and sewer service were needed, and the city was incorporated in July 1969. The same clay still affects plumbing today.

Clay holds water, drains slowly and swells and shrinks as it wets and dries. That movement stresses buried pipes, loads sump pits and keeps crawlspaces damp. For an emergency plumber, it means more sewer lateral damage, more crawlspace water and more slow drainfields than in sandier soil.

Emergency dispatch is available around the clock in Archdale. When a storm saturates the clay at 2 a.m. and the crawlspace starts filling, the 24/7 line is answered the same way it is at noon.

If A Pipe Bursts Or A Leak Starts In Archdale, Do This Before We Arrive

Heavy clay and low crawlspaces make slow water damage in Archdale easy to miss and hard to dry. The first ten minutes count.

1

Close the main water valve

Turn off the main at the meter box or where the line enters the house. Even when water seems to come from the ground, stopping the supply keeps the problem from growing.

2

Cut power to wet areas

Turn off breakers for the crawlspace, garage and any room with wet floors, since wiring in low spaces sits close to the water. Stay out of standing water.

3

Clear a path

Lift boxes off the floor, roll up rugs and open a clear route to the crawlspace access so the plumber can reach the problem fast.

4

Call the 24/7 line

Give your address and say whether the water looks like clean supply water, rainwater or sewage. If the home sits near the county line, mention it so the right permit office is used.

Two Counties

Archdale Sits On The Guilford-Randolph Line

The city extends from Randolph County north into Guilford County, and the ZIP code, 27263, is shared with parts of High Point. Which county an address is in decides which environmental health office holds its septic records and which inspections office handles permits. Mailing address does not always match the county.

If you are on septic, ask the county health department that covers your parcel for the permit and layout, then read about septic tank pumping. For homes on city sewer, clay-damaged laterals are the main issue, and sewer line repair or drain jetting addresses them.

Through The Seasons

Wet Springs, Hot Summers And Cold Snaps

Archdale's July highs average near 89 degrees and January lows average close to 30, according to local climate data. That range means pipes see both stress from summer heat and the occasional hard freeze. A freeze that holds for several hours can split a line in a crawlspace, so insulate exposed pipes before a forecast cold snap.

Heavy spring and summer rains, including tropical remnants, saturate the clay and push water toward foundations. A working sump pump with battery backup is the best protection, and it is worth testing before the wet season starts.

Homes in Archdale's older neighborhoods may also have cast-iron drains and galvanized supply lines, which corrode and shed rust. A water heater in a crawlspace or garage should be flushed yearly, because sediment from the supply settles at the bottom of the tank. Water heater trouble is covered under water heater repair.

Flooded Crawlspace

What To Do When Rain Floods Your Crawlspace

Water under the house can reach wiring and ductwork, so safety comes first.

  1. 1

    Stay out of the crawlspace if there is standing water near wiring, outlets or the electrical panel.

  2. 2

    Switch off power to the crawlspace circuits at the panel only if you can reach it while dry.

  3. 3

    If you have a sump pump, check that it is running and discharging away from the house.

  4. 4

    Photograph the water level and any damage for insurance.

  5. 5

    Call the 24/7 line and describe how deep the water is and where it is entering.
