Emergency Plumber In Randleman, NC 27317: What To Expect

Randleman is a Randolph County city south and a little east of High Point, past Archdale on the way toward Asheboro. The 24/7 emergency plumber line answers here for burst pipes, sewer backups, failed water heaters and slow drains that will not clear.

Randleman has a single ZIP code, 27317. That one code, home to roughly 17,500 people, spreads across about 54 square miles and also carries the mailing names Level Cross and New Salem, so a Randleman address can be a few blocks from city hall or several miles out on a country road. Dispatch asks for the street address because the plumbing on those two kinds of property is very different.

Emergency dispatch is available around the clock in Randleman, from streets near the town center to farms and subdivisions out toward Level Cross and New Salem. A water heater that fails at midnight gets the same response as a daytime call.

Burst Pipe Or Active Leak In A Randleman Home? Before We Arrive

Randleman homes range from town-sewer houses to country properties on septic tanks and wells. The first steps differ a little between them.

1

Shut the water off

On city water, close the main valve at the meter or where the line enters the house. On a well, switch off the pump breaker so the pressure tank stops feeding the leak.

2

Turn off power to wet areas

Switch off breakers for wet rooms. If water is near the water heater or a well pressure tank, leave the power off until a plumber has checked it.

3

Rest a septic system

When fixtures gurgle or back up, hold off on laundry, long showers and the dishwasher. A stressed tank and drainfield need time before more water goes in.

4

Call the 24/7 line

Give the street address and say whether the house is on a septic tank or a well. That decides the equipment sent.

Aging Water Heaters

Water Heaters From The 1990s Are Reaching Their Limit

A tank water heater usually lasts eight to twelve years, so a house built in the late 1980s or 1990s has probably been through at least one already. If the one in your garage or closet is past ten years, a leak at the base or rusty hot water is the usual warning. A tank that has failed at the bottom cannot be patched.

Professional water heater repair handles a bad element, thermostat or gas valve, while new water heater installation is the answer when the tank itself is leaking. Ask for the price before work begins, and compare it against the cost of waiting until the leak spreads.

Town Sewer Or Septic

Know Which System Your Randleman Property Uses

Addresses inside the city commonly connect to municipal sewer, while many parcels out toward Level Cross and New Salem use a private septic tank. A water bill with no sewer charge, and a concrete or plastic lid somewhere in the yard, point to septic. The county environmental health office holds the permit and the system layout for your parcel.

When every drain slows at once on septic, a full tank is a common cause, and septic tank pump-out is the routine fix. On town sewer, the same symptom points to the main line, which is a drain cleaning and unclogging job. A plumber can usually tell which one it is within the first few minutes.

Near The Water

Living Near Randleman Lake And The Deep River

Randleman sits on the Deep River, and Randleman Lake, the reservoir upstream of town, is part of the regional water supply. Low-lying yards near creeks and the lake can stay wet for days after heavy rain, and that moisture finds its way into crawlspaces. A wet crawlspace is a good place to look for a slow supply leak.

If the floor in a house on lower ground is damp after storms, a sump pump may be what keeps the space dry. A failed pump during a storm is an emergency call, and the 24/7 line takes it.
