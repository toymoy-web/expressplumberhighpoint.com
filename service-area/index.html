Where The Emergency Plumber Line Works

High Point sits where Guilford, Randolph, Forsyth and Davidson counties meet, and the service area follows that geography. The dispatcher covers the city itself and six nearby towns, each with its own page describing local housing, water and the problems residents call about.

Coverage is by address, not by town name alone. Rural parcels outside a town's limits are often covered too. If you are not sure, call the line and read out the street address.

Town Pages

Local Detail For Each Town

Jamestown is a Guilford County town built around the Oakdale Cotton Mill, with its own water and sewer system and an older mill village.

Archdale straddles the Guilford and Randolph county line, and its heavy clay soil is the reason the city built public water and sewer.

Trinity is a Randolph County city that began as the home of Trinity College, the school that became Duke University.

Thomasville, the Davidson County city known as Chair City, mixes older furniture-era buildings with newer subdivisions.

Randleman is a Randolph County city on a single ZIP code, 27317, that reaches out to the Level Cross and New Salem communities.

Greensboro is covered on its west and southwest side, the part of the city nearest High Point, where most homes date to the 1970s and 1980s.

Counties

Four Counties, Four Sets Of Offices

Septic rules are set statewide, in the standards of 15A NCAC 18A Section .1900, but each county runs its own environmental health office for permits and system records and applies them to its own parcels. Guilford County Environmental Health, for example, keeps septic permits for parcels in its part of Jamestown and Archdale, while Randolph County covers Trinity and the southern part of Archdale. Davidson and Forsyth counties cover Thomasville, Randleman and Greensboro.

That matters for planning. If your plumber needs a permit, or you need a copy of your septic layout, the right office depends on the exact parcel, not the mailing town. Archdale straddles the Guilford and Randolph county line, so ask the dispatcher or your plumber which office applies before filing anything. A permit filed with the wrong office is a common reason repairs get delayed, and a quick call at the start avoids it.

Water And Sewer

Water And Sewer Differ By Town

High Point draws its water from Oak Hollow Lake and Arnold Koonce City Lake, and city water is soft, with hardness in the published reports running roughly 30 to 60 mg/L. Jamestown built its own municipal water and sewer system in the years after its 1947 rechartering, and Archdale grew its public water and sewer partly because of the area's heavy clay soil.

Many homes outside the town sewer lines use septic tanks and private wells. If you are unsure which you have, your utility bill and the county septic permit tell you. The answer changes the plumbing advice, which is why every town page opens with the local setup before the repair details.
