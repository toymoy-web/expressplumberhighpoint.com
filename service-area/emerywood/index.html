About Emerywood

Plumbing In Emerywood, High Point

Emerywood is a residential neighborhood in High Point, between North Main Street and Westchester Drive. It is one of five early developments inside the Uptown Suburbs Historic District, which the National Register of Historic Places listed in 2013. Call the line when a leak, a clog or a dead water heater needs a plumber at an Emerywood address.

The area was platted in phases from 1922 to 1946 and covers about twenty blocks. Its streets curve with the land, and Hillcrest Drive follows the highest ridge. The National Register nomination notes that brick veneer is the most common exterior.

Streets And Directions

Giving A Plumber Directions On A Curving Street

Curving streets make a vague address costly. Give the full street address, the nearest cross street such as Westchester Drive or West Lexington Avenue, and the side of the house where the water meter or main valve sits. A plumber who knows where to park and where to start loses less time.

Homes in Emerywood went up across several decades. The NC Modernist site lists a 1949 house on Hillcrest Drive and a 1962 house on Emerywood Drive, so one neighborhood holds very different construction years. The date of a house says little about its pipes, because lines are often replaced piece by piece over the years. A plumber has to look at the actual pipe.

Before You Call

What To Have Ready When You Call From Emerywood

  1. 1

    Find the main shutoff

    Know where the main water valve is before you need it. If water is spreading, close it and call. Do not force a stuck valve.

  2. 2

    Check the meter

    With every fixture off, a meter dial that keeps moving points to a hidden leak. Mention it on the call.

  3. 3

    Describe what you see

    A wet ceiling, a damp wall stain, a slow drain and no hot water each call for a different first step. Say which one you have.

  4. 4

    Photograph the damage

    Take photos before cleanup. They help with an insurance claim and show the plumber how the problem started.

Plumbing Services For Emerywood Homes

These are general plumbing services, not a record of calls in Emerywood. A stain that spreads on a ceiling or a meter that never stops often leads to hidden leak detection. A slow main drain points to drain cleaning, and sewage in a floor drain calls for emergency sewer line repair.
