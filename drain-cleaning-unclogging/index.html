One Clog Or The Whole Line? A Two-Minute Test

The fastest way to sort a small clog from a serious one is to see how many fixtures are affected. A single slow sink is almost always a local clog in the trap or the branch pipe. A slow tub, a gurgling toilet and a washing machine that backs up together point to the main drain, and that is a different job.

Emergency drain cleaning is for the second case, plus any time sewage rises into a tub, shower or floor drain. Clogs in the main line do not clear on their own, and every flush or load of laundry adds to the problem. Stop using water and call the 24/7 line.

Local Pipes

Why High Point Drains Clog The Way They Do

Older High Point neighborhoods carry old drain materials. Houses in the Uptown Suburbs district, built from 1903 to 1963, often still have cast-iron stacks and clay sewer laterals. Cast iron rusts from the inside and grows rough scale that catches paper and grease. Clay joints let tree roots in, and Emerywood's mature, tree-lined streets give roots plenty of reason to look.

Mill houses near Highland Cotton Mills tend to have small-diameter branch lines patched over many decades. South-side ranches from the 1940s through the 1980s often combine cast iron under the slab with newer plastic added later, and the transitions are where debris snags. A camera inspection shows which of these you have before anyone recommends a repair.

Methods

Cabling Versus Hydro Jetting

Cabling (snaking)

  • Best for a single hard clog or a toy or wipe lodged in a line
  • Punches a hole through the blockage
  • Quick and well suited to branch lines
  • Can leave grease coating on the pipe walls

Hydro jetting

  • Best for grease, scale and root hair across the full pipe
  • Scours the walls with high-pressure water
  • Pairs well with a camera inspection
  • Not suitable for a collapsed or badly cracked pipe
What To Avoid

Why Chemical Drain Openers Make Things Worse

Store-bought drain openers can sit in a standing line for hours and soften old plastic joints or attack aging metal. If the opener does not clear the clog, the plumber then has to work around a pipe full of caustic liquid, which is a safety problem for the person on the other end of the cable.

Mention it on the call if you have used one. A plunger on a single fixture is fine. Repeated plunging on a toilet when the tub also gurgles only pushes the problem further down the line.

Before You Call

Do This While You Wait For The Plumber

A few steps can limit the mess and give the plumber useful information.

  1. 1

    Stop running water, flushing toilets and starting the dishwasher or washer.

  2. 2

    Note which fixtures are slow or gurgling, and in what order they started.

  3. 3

    Move rugs and stored items away from floor drains and the lowest bathroom.

  4. 4

    Locate the cleanout, a capped pipe in the yard, crawlspace or basement, if you can safely reach it. The International Plumbing Code (IPC), which the NC Plumbing Code is based on, calls for accessible cleanouts in drainage systems.

  5. 5

    Photograph any water that has come up, in case you file an insurance claim.

City Sewer Or Septic?

If You Are On Septic, A Slow Drain Can Mean Something Else

Many properties beyond High Point's sewer lines use a private septic tank. When every drain in a septic home slows down, a full tank or a clogged outlet filter is a common cause, and cabling the house line will not fix it. In that situation, septic pumping is the correct next step.

If the camera shows a damaged or root-filled pipe instead, the answer shifts to sewer lateral repair. Recurring grease and root problems are better handled by hydro jetting service, which cleans the full pipe wall rather than punching one hole. For a toilet that will not stop running or leaking after the drain is clear, see toilet repair.
