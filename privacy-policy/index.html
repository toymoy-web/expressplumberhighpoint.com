Overview

What This Policy Covers

This policy covers the website at expressplumberhighpoint.com and the phone line shown on it. The site has no contact forms, no accounts and no payment pages, so it collects far less than most websites.

In this policy, we means the operator of High Point Express Plumbing, an advertising and call-routing service. Calls are answered by independent local plumbing contractors.

Information

Information We Handle

When you call, the phone system records the caller's phone number, the time and length of the call, and the number you dialed. We may use call-tracking software to measure which pages lead to calls. If calls are recorded or monitored, you will be told at the start of the call, as the law requires.

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. The hosting provider keeps standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, for security and reliability.

Third Parties

Fonts, Maps And Other Services

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, and its pages include embedded Google Maps. When a page loads these, Google may receive your IP address and browser details, under its own privacy policy. The site does not use advertising cookies or social media pixels.

When you call, we share the call details with the contractor who answers so they can help you. We do not sell your personal information.

Use And Retention

How Information Is Used And Kept

We use call and email information to connect you with a contractor, respond to questions, measure performance and prevent abuse. We keep it only as long as needed for those purposes or as the law requires.

We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The site is not directed at them.

Your Choices

Access, Correction And Deletion

You can ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it or ask us to delete it, by emailing the address on the contact page. We will respond within a reasonable time and may need to confirm your identity first.

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated policy. See also the terms of service.

Privacy Questions?

Email the address on the contact page and the request will be reviewed.
