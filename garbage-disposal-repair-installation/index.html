What You Notice

What Your Garbage Disposal Is Telling You

Humming but not spinning

A jam is holding the flywheel.

Silent when switched on

A tripped reset, a dead outlet or a failed motor.

Leaks from the bottom

A cracked housing or failed seal means replacement.

Leaks from the top or side

A worn sink flange seal or loose drain connection.

Foul smell

Food residue in the grinding chamber or baffle.

Slow drain after grinding

A partial clog downstream or an overloaded disposal.

Emergency Disposal Repair

Emergency Disposal Repair: Leaks Under The Sink

A disposal is an emergency when it leaks into the cabinet, backs up the sink or floods the floor. Switch it off at the wall, unplug it if you can reach the cord and close the valves under the sink. Do not reach into the chamber with your hand.

A leak at the flange, the side drain or the dishwasher hose each calls for a different fix. Tell the dispatcher where the water appears and whether the unit is hardwired or plugged in. That helps the plumber bring the right gasket, hose or replacement unit.

Safe Steps

What To Do Before Calling About A Jam

Many jams clear in minutes. Safety comes first, because the disposal has sharp parts and strong torque.

  1. 1

    Switch the disposal off at the wall switch, and unplug it or turn off its breaker before reaching under the sink.

  2. 2

    Look into the chamber with a flashlight. Remove any visible object using tongs or pliers, never your hand.

  3. 3

    Find the hex socket in the center of the underside of the disposal, insert a hex key and work it back and forth to free the flywheel.

  4. 4

    Press the red reset button on the bottom of the unit once it has cooled.

  5. 5

    Restore power and run cold water, then test briefly. If it hums again, stop and call.

Decision

Repair Or Replace A Disposal

Repair works when

  • It is a simple jam or reset
  • The leak is at the flange or a drain connection
  • The unit is fairly new
  • The motor is strong

Replace when

  • The housing leaks at the bottom or seams
  • The motor has burned out
  • The unit is 8 to 12 years old
  • You want more power or quieter operation
Installing

What A Good Disposal Installation Includes

A proper installation connects the disposal to the sink flange, the drain trap and, if there is one, the dishwasher drain. The dishwasher inlet on a new disposal has a plug that must be knocked out, or the dishwasher will not drain. Skipping that step is one of the most common installation mistakes.

Power is the other piece. Wiring follows the National Electrical Code (NEC), and a disposal needs a switched outlet or a hardwired connection to a circuit that is not overloaded. Where wiring is needed, an electrician may be involved. Horsepower matters too, since a one-third horsepower unit jams more easily than a three-quarter one.

Septic Homes

Disposals And Septic Systems

Guilford County Environmental Health advises septic owners to limit the use of garbage disposals, because ground food adds solids to the tank. If your house is on septic, as many properties outside High Point's sewer lines are, a disposal shortens the time between pump-outs. Ask about the tank size and pumping schedule before installing one.

Homes on city sewer can use a disposal normally. Even so, grease, coffee grounds, fibrous vegetables and starchy rice do not belong in it. For septic care, see professional septic tank pumping.

When The Problem Is Not The Disposal

If water backs up in the sink even with the disposal off, the line downstream is blocked, and drain cleaning and unclogging is the fix. A leak under the sink that traces to the faucet or supply lines belongs with faucet and sink repair. Disposal service is also offered in Trinity and the other nearby towns.

Septic And Sewer

Disposals In Septic Homes And Mill-Village Kitchens

Many properties around Archdale, Trinity and Randleman use septic tanks, and a disposal adds solids that a tank has to process. In those homes it is worth asking whether a disposal belongs in the kitchen at all. County health guidance advises limiting disposal use on septic.

Kitchens in the Highland Cotton Mills Village and the Uptown Suburbs may have older drain lines with little slope. A new disposal on an old drain can slow the whole line, so the plumber checks the pipe beyond the trap before installing.

Leaks And Hookups

Where A Disposal Leaks And What The Dishwasher Has To Do With It

A leak under the sink can come from the flange at the top, the side drain, the dishwasher hose or the sink drain. A dishwasher that leaks at the air gap is a separate fix. Tell the plumber where the water shows and when, such as only while the dishwasher runs.

A replacement should include a new mounting kit, a check of the dishwasher connection and a leak test under the sink. If the sink backs up when the disposal runs, the clog may be in the drain past the disposal.
