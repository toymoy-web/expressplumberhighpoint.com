Is It Time

Signs A House Needs New Supply Lines

One leak is a repair. Three leaks in two years is a pattern. When the same house keeps producing pinholes, stains or failed fittings, the pipes are wearing out together, since they were installed together and aged together. Brown water on first use, weak flow at the showers and rust at the joints point the same way.

A repipe replaces the supply lines that carry water to every fixture. It does not touch the drains. Done well, it ends the repair cycle and clears the lowest-flow complaints in one project. A leak detection visit first can confirm whether the problem is localized or systemic.

Choosing New Pipe

PEX Versus Copper

PEX tubing

  • Flexible, so it snakes through walls with fewer openings
  • Resists pinholing and freeze splits better than rigid pipe
  • Usually the faster, lower-cost route
  • Needs protection from sunlight and some chemicals

Copper

  • Long record of durable service
  • Rigid, with more fittings and soldering
  • Higher material and labor cost
  • Can pinhole in some water chemistries
Local Water

Does High Point's Soft Water Affect Pipe Choice?

The city's published hardness readings run roughly 30 to 60 mg/L, which is soft. Water with very low mineral content can be more aggressive toward copper than hard water depending on its pH and alkalinity. If you are weighing copper, ask the plumber to read the pH and alkalinity numbers in the City of High Point's annual water quality report.

PEX does not corrode, so water chemistry matters far less. PEX tubing for drinking water should meet the ASTM F876 standard and the NSF/ANSI 61 standard, and the markings are printed on the pipe. That is one reason PEX has become the default for residential repiping across the Piedmont.

Local Housing

What Repiping Looks Like In Older High Point Homes

Houses in the Uptown Suburbs district, built from 1903 to 1963, are the most likely to still carry original galvanized lines. Plaster walls and hardwood floors make access harder, so a careful repipe uses small, planned openings and patches them. Mill houses near Highland Cotton Mills are smaller, which can shorten the job.

South-side ranches from the 1940s through the 1980s often have supply lines in the slab. Rather than breaking concrete, plumbers usually abandon those lines and re-route new ones through the attic or walls. If the slab itself is the concern, see slab leak repair.

Prepare

What To Do Before Repiping Day

The plumber handles the work. These steps make the day go smoothly.

  1. 1

    Clear furniture and stored items away from walls and closets where pipe will run.

  2. 2

    Plan for the water to be off for much of the day, and fill a few containers for drinking and flushing.

  3. 3

    Move pets and fragile decor out of the work area, since walls and ceilings will be opened in small spots.

  4. 4

    Ask who handles drywall patching and paint, because many plumbers leave patches ready for a finisher.

  5. 5

    Photograph every room before work starts, in case you need a record for insurance or a landlord.

Galvanized And Failing Lines

Galvanized Replacement And When A Repair Is Enough

Callers who ask about repiping often have galvanized pipe with low pressure, rusty water or repeated leaks. A single leak in an otherwise sound line may be repaired. Pipe that is scaled inside, failing at joints or leaking in several places usually calls for replacement.

Ask the plumber to show where the pipe is failing and to price both a repair and a repipe. CPVC and other plastic lines have their own failure patterns, so name your pipe material when you call and say how old the house is.
