An emergency plumber in High Point handles the plumbing failures that cannot wait for a regular appointment. A burst supply line, a sewage backup, a water heater leaking across the garage floor and a toilet that keeps overflowing all qualify. When To Call Right Away

What Counts As A Plumbing Emergency

Not every drip needs a midnight call. These six situations do, because the damage grows by the hour.

Burst or spraying pipe

A failed supply line can release gallons a minute into walls and ceilings. Close the main valve first, then call for burst pipe repair.

Sewage backup

Wastewater rising through floor drains or tubs is a health risk as well as a mess. Stop using water and ask about sewer line repair.

No hot water or a leaking tank

A leaking or dead heater usually needs water heater repair, or a replacement if the tank itself has rusted through.

Overflowing toilet or blocked main

When a toilet will not stop overflowing or every drain runs slow, the main line is usually blocked. Drain cleaning clears it.

Hidden leak, spinning meter

If the water meter keeps moving with every fixture off, water is escaping somewhere unseen. Leak detection finds it without opening walls at random.

Gas smell near an appliance

Leave the house and call 911 or the gas utility first. After the area is cleared, gas line repair can be scheduled.

Before The Plumber Arrives

The First Ten Minutes Of A Plumbing Emergency

What you do before help arrives often decides how much damage the house takes. This list works for most burst pipes and heater leaks.

  1. 1

    Close the main water valve by turning it clockwise. It is usually near the water heater, in the crawlspace or garage, or in a meter box near the curb.

  2. 2

    Turn off the water heater. Switch an electric unit off at the breaker and set a gas unit to its off or pilot setting. A heater running dry can damage itself.

  3. 3

    Stay out of standing water near outlets, cords and appliances. Cut power at the panel only if you can reach it while dry.

  4. 4

    Drain the lines. With the main closed, open the lowest faucet in the house and one upstairs to empty what is left in the pipes.

  5. 5

    Photograph the damage and the failed part before you move anything. Insurers and landlords usually ask for photos.

  6. 6

    Hold off on drains during a sewer backup. Do not flush or run sinks, tubs or washers, because every gallon you add has nowhere to go.

Worth Knowing

Soft Water Changes What Fails

High Point draws its water from Oak Hollow Lake and Arnold Koonce City Lake, and the city treats it at the Ward Water Plant. In several of the city's annual water quality reports published between 2016 and 2019, hardness readings ran roughly 30 to 60 mg/L. That is in the soft range, far below the scale-heavy water common in other parts of the country.

Soft water leaves less mineral scale on heating elements and inside tankless heat exchangers. Water heater trouble here tends to come from age, rusted tanks and failed valves rather than limescale. Most households do not need a softener to protect the heater, and a plumber should tell you so before selling one.

Taste and chlorine are separate questions. If those bother you, whole-home water filtration addresses them without the salt and upkeep of a softener.

Showrooms And Rentals

Market Weeks Raise The Stakes

High Point Market fills the city twice a year, in April and October. The Market Authority describes more than 11.5 million square feet of showroom space across roughly 180 buildings downtown, so a plumbing failure in the wrong week can close a showroom floor or cancel a guest's stay.

Property managers and short-term rental owners do better with a pre-Market walkthrough than with a midnight emergency. Check the shutoff valve under every sink, run each toilet through several flushes, and look at the base of the water heater for rust trails or moisture. A problem found a month before Market costs less than one found the night guests arrive.

Restaurants, offices and rental buildings with large water heaters or heavy kitchen drains can also book commercial plumbing service in the quiet weeks between shows.
