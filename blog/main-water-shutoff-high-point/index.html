Where Is My Main Water Shutoff In High Point?

In a leak, finding the main valve fast limits the damage. Do it now, while nothing is wrong.

Know Where The Valve Is Before You Need It

The main shutoff valve stops all water to the house. In a burst pipe, every minute with the water on adds damage. A valve you cannot find, or one that will not turn, turns a small leak into a large one.

The location depends on the house type. This guide shows where to look in the kinds of houses common around High Point, how to test the valve and what to do if it is stuck.

By House Type

These are common locations, not guarantees. Houses vary.

Find It

What To Do To Find And Test Your Main Valve

Do this with the water on and nothing leaking.

  1. 1

    Find where the water line enters the house. Check the garage, utility room and crawlspace access first.

  2. 2

    Look for a wheel handle (gate valve) or a lever handle (ball valve).

  3. 3

    Turn a wheel clockwise, slowly, until it stops. Turn a lever a quarter turn so it sits across the pipe.

  4. 4

    Open a faucet to confirm the water stops, then reopen the valve.

  5. 5

    Tag the valve and tell everyone in the household where it is.

The Meter Box

The Valve At The Meter And The Curb

Near the street, a meter box holds the water meter and usually a shutoff valve on the house side. A meter key or pliers can close it. The valve on the utility side of the meter belongs to the utility. Do not operate it, and do not tamper with the meter.

If you close the meter box valve, tell the plumber so they know the water was shut off at the street. If a leak is between the meter and the house, see water line repair and replacement.

Stuck Valves

Old Valves Seize, So Replace Them Before They Fail

Gate valves in older houses corrode and can break when forced. A valve that has not turned in years may seize, snap its stem or leak when moved. Exercise it once a year by closing and opening it gently, and replace one that is stiff or dripping.

Quarter-turn ball valves are more reliable and easier to use in a hurry. A plumber can swap a failing main valve in a short visit, as part of a fast burst pipe repair or on its own.

In An Emergency

What To Do When A Pipe Bursts

  1. 1

    Close the main

    Turn it off at the house valve or the meter box.

  2. 2

    Drain the lines

    Open the lowest faucet and one upstairs.

  3. 3

    Turn off the heater

    Shut off power or gas to the water heater.

  4. 4

    Call

    Describe where the water was coming from.

Share It

Label The Valve And Tell The Household

Put a bright tag on the main valve, take a photo of it with your phone and show everyone in the house how it works. Include teenagers, caregivers and guests who stay for a while. A landlord should give tenants the location in writing and put a label on the valve.

Practice once. Closing the valve and checking that a tap stops flowing takes two minutes and removes the panic if a real emergency happens.

Other Valves

Other Shutoffs Worth Knowing

Toilets and sinks have small valves, called stops, that cut water to just that fixture. The water heater has its own cold-water shutoff. Washing machine hoses connect to two valves behind the machine, and many houses have an indoor valve for outdoor spigots.

Know where the gas shutoff and the electrical panel are as well, but only use them if you can do so safely and the situation calls for it. For a gas smell, leave the house and call 911 or the gas utility.

Valve Types

Gate, Ball And Globe Valves Explained

A gate valve has a round wheel handle and closes with several turns. It is common in older houses and can corrode or break. A ball valve has a lever and closes with a quarter turn, so it works fast and reliably.

A globe valve is rarer for main service. The International Plumbing Code (IPC) calls for a full-open shutoff valve on the water service near where it enters the building. Knowing which one you have tells you how to close it.

If you turn a wheel handle and it spins freely, the stem may be broken inside the valve. Stop, use the meter box valve if you can reach it, and call a plumber to replace the main valve.

Next Steps

Make It A Household Habit

Test the main valve once a year and check the labels on other shutoffs. Replace any valve that is stiff, dripping or corroded before it fails. Make sure a spare meter key or a pair of pliers is where you can find it. When you move into a new house, finding the shutoff should be on the first-week list, next to locating the electrical panel and the smoke detectors.

At The Meter

Finding The Meter And Who Owns Which Valve In High Point

The City of High Point's lead-free page includes a graphic on how to locate your water meter, which helps if the meter box is buried in grass. The valve on the utility side of the meter belongs to the city, so do not operate it. For a problem at the meter, call the Customer Service Center at (336) 883-3111, listed as open 24 hours a day.

Tag the house-side valve and show everyone in the home where it is. If a pipe bursts and the valve will not close, call the burst pipe repair line. Homeowners in Archdale and Jamestown should locate their own meters and ask their utility who owns the valve.
