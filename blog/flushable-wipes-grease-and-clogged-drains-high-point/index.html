Flushable Wipes, Grease And Clogged Drains In High Point

Most drain clogs trace to a short list of habits. Knowing them is the cheapest plumbing advice there is.

The Short List

A Few Habits Cause Most Clogs

Plumbers see the same culprits again and again: wipes, cooking grease, hair, soap film, coffee grounds, fibrous food scraps and the occasional toy. A drain is a one-way pipe built for water and toilet paper, and everything else collects somewhere. The place it collects is usually a bend, a joint or a rough patch of old cast iron. This guide covers the worst offenders, what to do instead and when a clog is a sign of a deeper problem.

Offenders

What Clogs Drains And What To Do Instead

The right habit depends on the fixture.

Wipes

Why Flushable Wipes Are Not Flushable

Toilet paper breaks apart quickly in water. Most wipes, including those labeled flushable, hold together. They snag on rough pipe walls, catch more waste and form dense mats that can block a main line or a pump. Utilities across the country ask customers to flush only human waste and toilet paper.

A toilet that takes a wipe once may clear. The wipe then travels to a bend in the line and waits for company. If you have already flushed many, a camera inspection can show what is in the line.

Grease

Grease Does Not Stay Liquid

Hot fat flows down a drain easily, then cools in the pipe and sticks to the walls. Over months it narrows the line, catches food and turns into a hard coating. Hot water and dish soap push it a few feet farther down the line, which only delays the problem.

Pour cooled grease into a container and trash it. Wipe pans with a paper towel before washing. A kitchen line that clogs repeatedly may need professional hydro jetting to strip the buildup.

Fix It Right

What To Do When A Drain Starts Slowing

Try the simple steps first.

  1. 1

    Use a plunger on a single clogged fixture, with a tight seal and steady strokes.

  2. 2

    Remove and clean the sink or tub stopper and the visible trap area.

  3. 3

    Avoid chemical drain openers, which can sit in old pipe and make a plumber's job hazardous.

  4. 4

    If several fixtures are slow or gurgle, stop and call. The main line is the issue.

  5. 5

    If sewage is rising, stop all water use and call right away.

Older Pipes

Why Old Triad Plumbing Clogs Faster

Cast-iron drains in houses from the Uptown Suburbs and the 1940s to 1980s ranches develop rough scale that catches debris. Clay laterals with root-filled joints do the same. A smooth new pipe forgives bad habits more than a rough old one does.

If clogs keep returning, the pipe may be the problem, not your habits. Drain cleaning service clears the line, a camera shows why, and garbage disposal repair helps if the disposal is part of the chain. Homeowners in Trinity on septic should be extra careful, since solids fill the tank faster.

Kitchen

Kitchen Habits That Protect Your Lines

Scrape plates and pans into the trash before washing. Wipe greasy pans with a paper towel. Keep a small container for cooled fat and throw it away when full. Use a sink strainer to catch food scraps, and run cold water briefly when you use a garbage disposal.

Rinsing a pan with hot water does not solve the grease problem. The fat travels down the line and cools. Over time the coating narrows the pipe, and a plumber has to scour it off with a jetter.

Bathroom

Bathroom Habits And Septic Homes

Only toilet paper and human waste belong in the toilet. Wipes of any kind, cotton swabs, dental floss, hair and feminine products belong in the trash. A small trash can with a lid near the toilet makes that easy.

Septic owners have another reason to be careful. Solids fill the tank faster, and wipes can clog the outlet filter or the pump. According to Guilford County Environmental Health, septic owners should keep chemicals, cleaning solutions and grease out of the system.

Everyone In The House

Teaching Kids, Guests And Tenants

Most clogs start with someone who did not know. A small sign in the bathroom, a lidded trash can and a short note for guests and tenants prevent more calls than any product. Children flush toys, and visitors flush wipes, so make the right habit the easy one.

Landlords can add a line to the lease about what may be flushed. Short-term hosts can post a brief house guide with the shutoff location and the trash rule. A few lines of instruction are far cheaper than a service call.

Next Steps

When Habits Are Not Enough

If you have changed your habits and drains still clog, the line itself is the problem. Rough cast iron, root-filled clay joints and sagging pipe all catch debris no matter how careful you are. A camera inspection shows which one you have.

From there the choices are simple: clear the line, scour it with a jetter, repair a damaged section or replace the line. Each is a different cost and a different level of durability, and a plumber can explain them with the footage in front of you.

In Septic And Sewer Homes

Why It Matters Even More Outside The Sewer Lines

Many properties around Trinity, Archdale and rural Guilford and Randolph counties are on septic tanks. A wipe that survives the trip through a sewer main can still lodge in a tank outlet or a pump chamber. Fat and food solids shorten the time between pump-outs too.

If you are unsure which system you have, assume the careful habits apply. They cost nothing and protect both the plumbing and the yard.

Your Pipe Or The Main

Who To Call When The Whole Street Seems Backed Up

If every drain in the house backs up at once, the blockage may be in your service line or in the main in the street. The City of High Point's Customer Service Center, listed at (336) 883-3111 and open 24 hours a day, is the number to call if you think the main is involved. The city also posts wastewater spill notices on its website.

A plumber handles anything in your own pipes, from emergency drain cleaning to a camera check. Keeping wipes and grease out of the line is cheaper than either call. Households in Thomasville and Trinity should use their own utility's number for the street main.
